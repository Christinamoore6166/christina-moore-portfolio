@AGENTS.md

PROJECT: Personal portfolio site for Christina Moore. Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind + shadcn/ui. Deployed on Vercel free tier. A single scrolling page.

PURPOSE: A creative portfolio — event branding, cake decorating, crafting, graphic design — framed so that someone hiring for strategy or program management roles sees the professional through-line: creativity as a differentiator inside corporate work. It is not a resume and it is not a design agency site.

SECTIONS in order: Hero, 01 About Me, 02 Experience, 03 Branding / Event Planning, 04 Other (cake decorating, crafting and customized gifts, graphic design gallery), Contact / Footer.

RULES:
- All copy lives in src/content/site.ts. Never hardcode copy in a component.
- All images live under public/images/<section>/ and are referenced through src/content/images.ts, never by literal path in JSX.
- Motion is restrained: scroll-reveal fades, hover states, and at most one slow parallax on the hero. No autoplaying video, no WebGL, no carousels.
- Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion.
- Mobile first. Check every section at 390px before calling it done.
- Search the 21st dev registry for a component before hand-writing UI. But judge the result by what it actually is: a pure layout (grid, collage, split panel) is a few lines of CSS and near-zero risk to hand-build against this design system's shape rules — reject a registry match there on style grounds without a second thought. A component with real interaction behavior (lightbox focus trapping, escape handling, scroll locking, arrow-key navigation, focus restore on close; also comboboxes, date pickers, carousel-style state machines) is not cheap to get right — judge those on whether the behavior is solid, not on whether the default styling matches, since radius and shadow are trivially overridden but correct focus management is not something to retrofit casually.
- Use design tokens from tailwind.config and globals.css. Never a raw hex value inside a component.
- SHAPE (Sep 2026, replaces the earlier no-radius/no-shadow rule): rounded corners (radius-card) belong on photo cards, glass panels, the lightbox image and pill controls only. A soft shadow belongs on glass surfaces only — photo cards get no shadow. Full-bleed and backdrop photography stays square and anchored to the edges. Nothing else gets a radius or a shadow.
- Static site only — no database, no API routes, no server-side data fetching.
- Ask before adding any new dependency.
- PROJECT ROOT is C:\dev\christina-moore-portfolio. Never create or write files outside it. If a session starts in C:\dev or anywhere else, stop and tell me before doing anything.
- This is Next.js 16 with the App Router. Follow Next 16 conventions; do not carry over patterns from earlier versions.
- Alt text in src/content/images.ts is PROVISIONAL. It was generated by cycling a small bank of event-level descriptions across every photo, and only about 30 of roughly 130 images were actually looked at. Treat any alt text as unverified until you have viewed that specific image. Never describe a photo you have not seen.
- CONTACT is LinkedIn only. Never render an email address or a mailto: link anywhere on the site, in metadata, or in JSON-LD.
- GALLERY GRAMMAR is one repeating module, EventFeature (feature image plus numbered strip). Do not introduce a second gallery layout without asking.
- STOCK imagery lives in public/images/stock/ and is only ever a tinted backdrop. It never appears inside a strip, the tile marquee, or the lightbox.
- Every string in the Experience section renders from src/data/resume.ts and nothing else.

SETTLED DEVIATIONS FROM THE ROADMAP (Sep 2026)
These five differ from docs/Visual-Enhancement-Roadmap.md on purpose. They
were reviewed against the live site and accepted. Do not "fix" them back.
- The Experience timeline rule draws in one shot on entry, not tied to
  scroll progress with animation-timeline: view(). The roadmap named the
  one-shot as the fallback; it is the shipped version.
- Experience tab panels have no outgoing cross-fade. The incoming panel
  reveals with its own stagger; the outgoing one is simply replaced.
- The Skills tab is a stat grid, not animated skill bars. There were never
  any levels to show, so there is nothing for a bar to represent.
- The closing CTA panel is glass-dark, not glass. It sits over a tinted
  linen photo, where light glass loses the text.
- There is no <meta name="view-transition"> tag. It governs cross-document
  transitions only; the showcase's document.startViewTransition() works
  without it.
